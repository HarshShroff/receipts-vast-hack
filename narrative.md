1. The Hook (20s)
- 2.5 million workplace injuries and illnesses reported in the U.S. in 2024.
- Cameras record warehouses, hospitals, factories, and construction sites.
- But investigating an incident still means searching through hours of footage.
- What if you could ask your cameras what happened and get proof?

2. The Solution: RECEIPTS (15s)
- RECEIPTS: The video AI agent with memory.
- Remembers what cameras see and tracks changes over time.
- Every answer comes with a timestamp and video clip.
- Knows when evidence is missing or outdated.

3. Live Walkthrough (70s)
Scenario: You're a warehouse security supervisor.
Step A: What's happening now?
- You're monitoring a busy warehouse aisle.
- Ask: "Is the forklift moving?"
- Get an answer and click the supporting video clip.
Step B: What happened earlier?
- Someone reports a potential safety incident.
- Go back in time.
- Ask: "Was a worker present?"
- Get the answer and exact footage.
Step C: What changed?
- Advance the timeline.
- Ask: "Has the worker left the aisle?"
- See how the answer updates with new evidence.
Step D: What if the camera stops?
- Move beyond the available footage.
- Ask about the current situation.
- RECEIPTS flags the answer as stale.
- "There's a difference between knowing it was safe and knowing it is safe."
Step E: How we built it
- NVIDIA Cosmos Reason: Understands what's happening in video.
- VAST DataEngine: Processes and indexes video observations.
- CoreWeave: Provides GPUs for model inference.
- Cursor: Used to build and develop the application.
- Weights & Biases: Tracks experiments and evaluation results.
